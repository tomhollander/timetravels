About

Welcome to Time Travels! I love looking at old maps, and I hope you do too. This site makes it super easy to navigate through different map pages and compate the same area acrcoss different years.

The site is free to use and free from ads, but if you want to show your appreciation, you can buy me a coffee at Ko-Fi.

If you have any of your own out-of-copyright maps you'd like to contribute, you can contact me at timetravels.au@outlook.com.

Credits and Attributions
